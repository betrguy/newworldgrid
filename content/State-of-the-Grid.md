<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">The US grid's transformation is marked by the rapid deployment of large-scale battery storage and integration of renewable energy sources, accelerating the transition to low-carbon infrastructure. This momentum is exemplified by the addition of a battery capacity equivalent to 20 nuclear reactors in just four years.</p>

As global tensions escalate, it is crucial to emphasize the critical role of grid resilience in absorbing physical or political shocks. The development of Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) for microgrid applications highlights their potential to provide stable power during times of uncertainty.
<div class="sotg-bullets">
<ul>
  <li><strong>Why it matters:</strong> The US grid's transformation away from fossil fuels and towards renewable energy sources has significant implications for national security.</li>
  <li><strong>The big picture:</strong> This shift could fundamentally alter the global balance of power by reducing reliance on foreign oil and increasing energy independence, potentially changing the dynamics of international relations.</li>
  <li><strong>What to watch:</strong> Monitor the development and deployment of Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) for microgrid applications as a key indicator of grid resilience and national security.</li>
</ul>
