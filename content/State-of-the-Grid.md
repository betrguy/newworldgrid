<p class="meridian-lede">The rapid deployment of utility-scale batteries in the United States has accelerated the grid's transition towards a more resilient and decentralized architecture. This momentum is further underscored by Australia's remarkable pace of renewable energy integration, which now rivals the output of 20 nuclear reactors.</p>

In light of escalating global tensions and fuel supply concerns, it is essential to emphasize the critical role of grid resilience in absorbing physical or political shocks. The recent advancement of Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) for microgrid applications highlights their potential to provide stable power during times of uncertainty.
