<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">The US grid's transformation is marked by a significant increase in solar and wind power inputs, necessitating decentralized and regionalized energy management. Transmission operators are prioritizing uplift and ancillary services to ensure reliability, underscoring the importance of robust cyber defenses.</p>

As the grid continues to integrate intermittent renewables, the development of nuclear batteries offers a promising solution for dispatchable power generation. This technology has the potential to provide reliable backup power without requiring new transmission lines or energy storage infrastructure upgrades.
