<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">The grid's physical reality has been underscored by a recent cyberattack on the US power grid, highlighting the importance of infrastructure resilience in the face of potential disruptions. The attack demonstrates that even a single scenario involving nine compromised transformers could cause widespread outages.</p>

In response to these concerns, the focus on decentralized energy storage and microgrid applications is gaining momentum. Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) are being explored as a low-carbon solution for reliable power delivery, particularly in microgrid settings.
