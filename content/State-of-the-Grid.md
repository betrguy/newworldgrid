This initiative underscores the growing importance of nuclear power in meeting energy demands sustainably.</p>

Paragraph 2: Spain’s plan to allocate 12.4GW of grid capacity for green hydrogen aligns with efforts to integrate renewable energy into the grid, enhancing stability and reducing dependency on fossil fuels. This move supports the broader narrative of utilities, markets, and regulators investing in new generation and demand response technologies to maintain grid stability.

  <li><strong>The big picture:</strong> These moves reinforce the transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy, potentially reshaping global energy markets and geopolitical dynamics.</li>
  <li><strong>What to watch:</strong> The integration of green hydrogen into the grid and its impact on energy stability and dependency.</li>
