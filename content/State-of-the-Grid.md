<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">The U.S. now accounts for nearly 40% of global data center electricity consumption due to the surge in AI-driven workloads, straining the grid and necessitating new infrastructure investments. This shift underscores the growing importance of data center efficiency and the need for enhanced grid resilience to accommodate the increasing load.</p>

The geopolitical implications of heightened data center energy demands are significant, as they can impact grid stability and reliability. The U.S. military's reliance on data to guide operations, as evidenced by recent incidents, highlights the vulnerability of critical infrastructure to cyber and physical threats. As space data centers proliferate, they could disrupt traditional stargazing and introduce new risks, further complicating the balance between technological advancement and environmental considerations.

  <li><strong>The big picture:</strong> This shift underscores the growing intersection of technology and geopolitics, with implications for grid stability and military operations.</li>
  <li><strong>What to watch:</strong> The development of grid resilience technologies and their deployment in critical infrastructure sectors.</li>
</ul>
