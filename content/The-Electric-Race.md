## Daily Electric Brief — Mar 20, 2026

**Today's Briefing**

Tesla and Chinese manufacturers are rewriting the EV endgame, with China's battery supply chain and grid integration driving innovation. A breakthrough in sodium-ion batteries offers 4C fast charging in just 11 minutes, while GM reports that its Ultium platform will deliver 500-mile range via lithium-metal chemistry. The American market is focused on commercial and fleet sales, with Ford hemorrhaging money on EV production and struggling to build out its charging network.

---

**The American Front**

* **Ford**: Its priority is winning the commercial and fleet market in North America, where China has not yet dominated. Ford needs to close the gap on EV production costs and establish a credible charging network.
* **GM**: GM's strategic priority is delivering a single, deliverable win in the Ultium battery platform, which it has struggled to execute. It must overcome software and charging infrastructure weaknesses.
* **Tesla**: Tesla's priority is staying ahead of BYD on cost per kWh while defending its Supercharger network as the de facto U.S. standard.
