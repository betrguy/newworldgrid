## Daily Electric Brief — Mar 18, 2026

**Today's Briefing**

Washington has opened Tesla’s direct EV sales loophole to Rivian and Lucid, expanding EV adoption in the US market. Companies are developing ultra-fast charging systems that can refill electric vehicle batteries in minutes. In China, CATL and BYD hold leading positions in the LFP battery market, with CATL ranking first with 49.79% market share.

Rapid-charging EV batteries are on the way: Companies are developing ultra-fast charging systems that can refill electric vehicle batteries in minutes.

**The American Front**

**Ford**: Ford's priority is to commercialize its F-150 Lightning and establish a credible charging network, leveraging its truck-first install base. However, Ford has hemorrhaged money on EV production and lacks a substantial charging infrastructure.

**GM**: GM needs a single, deliverable win in the EV market. It has made significant bets on battery technology with Ultium but has struggled with execution, ceding software and charging infrastructure to other companies. Its Cruise autonomous unit imploded, leaving GM without a clear alternative to China's EV lead.

**Tesla**: Tesla's priority is not catching up with BYD on cost per kWh, but defending its Supercharger network as the de facto US standard while building its global footprint. With ownership of both vehicle and grid assets, Tesla has a unique advantage in driving the US EV market forward.

**Scenario 1: Charging Network Innovator**
**EcoCycle**: EcoCycle aims to create a comprehensive network of fast-charging stations along highways, using AI-optimized routes and real-time traffic data to minimize wait times. By integrating with existing EV fleets, EcoCycle can accelerate widespread adoption of rapid charging.

**Scenario 2: Grid Services Provider**
**VoltEdge**: VoltEdge focuses on developing a decentralized grid services platform that enables peer-to-peer energy trading between EV owners and the grid. By leveraging blockchain technology and AI-powered forecasting, VoltEdge can optimize energy distribution and reduce strain on traditional grids.

**Scenario 3: Closed-Loop Mineral Supply Chain**
**Minera**: Minera targets the development of a closed-loop mineral supply chain for EV battery production, utilizing advanced recycling technologies to recover valuable materials from spent batteries. By reducing dependence on primary mining, Minera can help mitigate environmental and social concerns associated with large-scale battery production.
