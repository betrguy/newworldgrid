## Daily Electric Brief — Mar 18, 2026

**Today's Briefing**

Washington has opened Tesla’s direct EV sales loophole to Rivian and Lucid, allowing them to sell electric vehicles directly to consumers without needing a traditional dealership. CATL and BYD continue to dominate China's electric vehicle battery market, with CATL beating estimates as its battery profit growth quickens. Meanwhile, the build-out of public charging points in the US is expected to accelerate globally, with over 30% increase in fast-charging ports in the U.S. alone.

**The American Front**

**Ford**: Ford's priority should be to stabilize its EV production and commercialize its F-150 Lightning platform, which remains a significant advantage in the North American market. However, its lack of a credible charging network and high production costs pose significant risks to its competitiveness. The company needs to address these weaknesses to challenge China's dominance in the US market.

**GM**: GM's focus should be on delivering a single, deliverable win with its Ultium battery platform, which has shown promise but has been marred by execution issues. The company must demonstrate the scalability and efficiency of its battery technology to regain ground lost to Chinese competitors. A major breakthrough is needed to reassert GM's position in the global EV market.

**Tesla**: Tesla's priority remains defending its charging network as the de facto U.S. standard while China builds its own global footprint. The company needs to maintain its cost per kWh leadership and continue to expand its Supercharger network to support growing demand for electric vehicles. Its focus should be on staying ahead of BYD, rather than simply catching up.

**Thought Exercise: Three Startups That Could Leap China**

* **Charged Networks**: A startup developing advanced thermal adhesives for battery cell-to-pack and cell-to-ribbon performance enhancement in electric vehicles. The technology aims to increase energy density and reduce charging times.
* **GridSpark**: A company focused on developing a blockchain-based platform for recycling EV batteries, with the goal of creating a more efficient and sustainable supply chain for critical materials like lithium and cobalt.
* **Volta Minerals**: A startup working on advanced lithium mining technologies, including a novel extraction method that reduces energy consumption by 30% compared to traditional methods. The technology has the potential to disrupt the global lithium market and support Western EV manufacturers in their bid to close the cost gap with China.
