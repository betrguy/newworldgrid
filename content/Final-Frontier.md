## Frontier Brief — Mar 31, 2026

**The High Ground**

Axiom Space's orbital compute node has ignited a revolution, supplanting traditional LEO constellations and catering to skyrocketing demand for advanced computational resources in orbit. This breakthrough transcends previous space-based solutions, empowering unparalleled cloud computing and AI/ML capabilities.

In a stunning turn of events, Axiom's orbital data center nodes are now free-flying, offering dedicated infrastructure for cloud computing, AI/ML, data fusion, and space cybersecurity applications.

### **The EV Race**

The US is losing its grip on the global electric vehicle (EV) market as China rapidly gains ground. Gas prices may have stabilized, but the writing's on the wall: with governments worldwide setting ambitious carbon reduction targets and consumers increasingly demanding eco-friendly options, the internal combustion engine's days are numbered. The math speaks for itself: with Chinese EVs expected to bolster electrification across markets in 2026, it's only a matter of time before BYD Auto and Tesla face stiff competition from homegrown American innovators – or risk being left in the dust.

*What follows is purely a thought experiment. This is a forward-looking scenario by ARC.*

Imagine a world where the proliferation of orbital data centers and satellite-based infrastructure enables a seismic shift in global energy markets. As these hubs become more prevalent, they could begin to "see" patterns in energy consumption that traditional ground-based systems have not detected before. Envision a future where these orbital vantage points facilitate a sudden, 0.1% increase in global energy efficiency, achieved by optimizing power distribution and grid management.
