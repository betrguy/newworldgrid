### **The High Ground**

Amazon's launch of its Kuiper broadband internet constellation further solidifies its role in the orbital economy. Russia successfully launched an Angara-1.2 carrier rocket with an unknown payload, highlighting the significance of reliable and efficient space-based infrastructure for critical objectives.

### **The EV Race**

BYD's announcement of their Gen 2 Blade battery with a peak charging capacity of 1,000 Wh is significant, as it may impact the electric vehicle market. This development does not provide any immediate implications for the automotive industry, but rather serves as a potential game-changer in the long run.

### **Frontier Speculation: The 0.1% Shift**

What follows is purely a thought experiment. In 2029, Amazon's Kuiper internet constellation, now a de facto global infrastructure, triggers an unexpected consequence. China's BYD Co., having invested heavily in EV production to supply the expanding global market, discovers that its batteries are being hacked by rogue nation-state actors, using the Kuiper network as a backdoor. This revelation forces BYD to redirect its resources from mass production to develop an AI-powered battery monitoring and security system, effectively creating a new market for trusted EV battery cybersecurity solutions. As a result, Russia's space agency takes notice of this emerging niche and begins developing its own Angara-1.2 rocket-based satellite platform specifically designed for secure data transfer in the Kuiper network.
