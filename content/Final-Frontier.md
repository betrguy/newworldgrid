### **The High Ground**

Artemis crew members have safely returned to Earth after their nine-day voyage around the Moon. This journey took them further from Earth than any humans in history.

### **The EV Race**

The number of 500-kW V4 Tesla Superchargers has increased to seven, according to EVChargingStations.com. However, this expansion has not translated into improved market performance for Tesla or its peers, as evidenced by a recent 30% dip in Tesla stock that rippled across the industry, as reported by The Globe and Mail.

**Frontier Speculation: The 0.1% Shift**
*What follows is purely a thought experiment.*

Imagine a scenario where the successful Artemis program sparks an unexpected chain reaction in the electric vehicle (EV) market. With NASA's lunar missions generating unprecedented international interest and investment, SpaceX's Starship program receives a massive influx of funding to accelerate its development timeline. This surge in capital allocation enables SpaceX to rapidly scale its battery production capacity, driving down costs by 20%. As a result, Tesla's core business model is severely disrupted, leading to a sharp correction in the company's stock price. Suddenly, investors are scrambling to pivot their portfolios towards more resilient EV plays, such as Rivian and NIO.
