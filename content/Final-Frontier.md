## Frontier Brief — Mar 27, 2026

**The High Ground**

CNBC reports that low Earth orbit is attracting billions in investment due to its potential support for orbital data centers, geospatial intelligence, and autonomous space operations. Axiom Space's first operational LEO compute node targets Q1 2027, with thousands of satellites planned to follow. This development shifts the technical landscape by introducing a new tier of latency-sensitive applications, enabling cloud computing, AI/ML, data fusion, and space cybersecurity applications.

As orbital data centers take shape, the focus expands from narrow LEO constellations to a broader set of applications. The value proposition has expanded exponentially, ensuring significant investment attraction as it enables novel use cases.
