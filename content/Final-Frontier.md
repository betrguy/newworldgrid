## Frontier Brief — Mar 27, 2026

**The High Ground**

Today's signal of note is the launch of the Starship HLS (Human Landing System) by SpaceX, which marks a significant milestone for lunar landing capabilities. This development shifts the strategic balance by providing a reliable and reusable option for human exploration of the Moon. With this capability, SpaceX can now focus on establishing a sustainable presence in space, potentially disrupting the traditional model of launching expensive and heavy payloads to orbit.

The Starship HLS is also a key step towards enabling the deployment of orbital data centers (ODCs), which will provide low-latency computing capabilities in orbit. This technology has the potential to revolutionize industries such as finance, healthcare, and transportation by providing access to high-performance computing from space.

The cost implications of this development are significant, with the ability to launch payloads to the Moon and establish a sustainable presence in space potentially reducing costs compared to traditional approaches.

**The Terrestrial Engine**

China's BYD has seen its first profit drop since 2021, despite still maintaining its position as a leading electric vehicle (EV) manufacturer. This development highlights the intense competition in the EV market, with BYD and Tesla vying for dominance. The US remains ahead of China in terms of EV technology and manufacturing capabilities, with companies such as General Motors and Ford investing heavily in EV production.

The profit drop at BYD also underscores the challenges facing the global EV supply chain, including issues related to battery production and sourcing. The US and Europe are leading the charge in terms of EV technology development, while China is focusing on expanding its market share through aggressive pricing and marketing strategies.

**Frontier Speculation: The 0.1% Shift**

What if the widespread adoption of space-based solar power (SBSP) enabled by NASA's SBSP warning becomes a reality? With the ability to harness energy from space and beam it back to Earth, the need for traditional fossil fuel-based infrastructure could be significantly reduced. This could enable a global transition to clean energy, with the cost of electricity potentially dropping by as much as 20-30% due to reduced transmission losses.
