## Frontier Brief — Mar 27, 2026

**The High Ground**

Orbital data centers are rewriting the rules of cloud computing and AI/ML capabilities in space. Axiom Space's LEO compute node is at the forefront, with thousands of satellites planned for launch. This seismic shift moves beyond narrow LEO constellations to cater to soaring demand for advanced computational resources in orbit.

**The EV Race**
As gas prices surge across the US, interest in electric vehicles jumps 20%, a seismic shift that's rewriting the playbook for Tesla and BYD. China's BYD has dropped its profit margins, but still leads in global sales as it leverages manufacturing scale and aggressive pricing to outmaneuver Elon Musk's Tesla. The market is no longer driven by early adopters; consumers are now running the math on their wallet. The calculus is simple: with gas prices soaring, EVs' long-term cost savings become impossible to ignore. As battery breakthroughs like "miracle" batteries gain traction, the US-China competition will only intensify.

*What follows is purely a thought experiment. This is a forward-looking scenario by ARC.*

Imagine a world where BYD's unexpected profit drop sparks a chain reaction in the global EV ecosystem. As Tesla, sensing an opportunity, rapidly accelerates the development of its own high-density battery tech to undercut BYD and maintain market share. Meanwhile, Chinese government entities, suddenly concerned about the domestic EV industry's vulnerability, secretly authorize a massive investment in urban infrastructure – not just charging stations, but also entirely new vertical cities, where ceiling-crawling robots like those unveiled by the Chinese company would be integrated into daily life. These "smart" structures could become hubs for decentralized energy trading and peer-to-peer EV charging, bypassing traditional grid infrastructure and disrupting the entire fossil fuel supply chain.
