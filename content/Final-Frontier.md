### **The High Ground**

SpaceX seeks permission to launch a constellation of up to one million satellites that will act as orbital data centers, according to recent reports from SpaceX. To achieve this massive undertaking, a significant increase in launch throughput and orbital infrastructure requirements is necessary.

### **The EV Race**

Airline restrictions on portable chargers have been introduced to mitigate fire risks, effectively limiting travelers' ability to carry multiple charging devices on flights (Source: [battery supply]). This shift may prompt electric vehicle buyers to reconsider their charger needs and potentially prioritize vehicles with integrated charging solutions over those relying solely on external portability.

*What follows is purely a thought experiment.*

In 2029, a global shortage of critical lithium minerals for electric vehicle batteries creates an unexpected ripple effect in the emerging orbital economy. Elon Musk's SpaceX, desperate to secure a reliable supply chain for its constellation project, partners with NIO, one of the top startup-focused EV brands, to vertically integrate production of high-capacity battery cells within their respective satellite-based data centers. This sudden shift enables Rivian and Lucid Motors, also in the NIO alliance, to accelerate their own electric vehicle manufacturing processes, cornering 80% of the global market share by 2030.
