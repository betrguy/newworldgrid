### **Frontier Speculation: The 0.1% Shift**

*What follows is purely a thought experiment.*

As the Loft Orbital and EarthDaily launch sets a new standard for satellite deployment, I propose an unexpected outcome. In 2032, BYD Co.'s rapid EV market expansion creates a peculiar supply chain consequence. With Tesla struggling to meet demand, they turn to CATL, the Chinese battery giant, to secure priority production slots. This partnership prompts a rare but significant shift in global trade flows: China, now a dominant force in electric vehicle manufacturing, begins secretly diverting excess lithium and nickel from their vast mining operations to support Loft Orbital's satellite constellation build-out. As this "industrial-scale" satellite component supply chain takes hold, it would have far-reaching implications for international relations, resource allocation, and the future of Earth's orbital infrastructure.
