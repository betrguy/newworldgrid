Amazon launches first Kuiper internet satellites, taking on Starlink

Amazon has launched its first 27 satellites for its Kuiper broadband internet constellation into space from Florida.

**The EV Race**

BYD's stock surged to an all-time high after unveiling a five-minute charging battery technology that outperforms Tesla's capabilities. This breakthrough has significant implications for buyers and manufacturers alike.

*What follows is purely a thought experiment.*

Imagine 2029: Indonesia's aggressive push to capture the EV battery supply chain through CATL and Hyundai has paid off in a big way. The sudden influx of affordable, high-performance batteries has enabled BYD to not only maintain its market share but also expand into previously inaccessible markets, including those with lax environmental regulations. This snowball effect leads to an unexpected consequence: BYD's dominance in the EV market allows it to dictate the terms of battery recycling and reuse protocols, effectively cornering the market on secondary lithium sources. As a result, Amazon's Kuiper constellation, relying heavily on these recycled materials for its satellite production, faces an unprecedented shortage of essential components, crippling its ability to scale up its internet services in the orbit.
