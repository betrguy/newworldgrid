**The High Ground**

NASA's accelerated launch of the Nancy Grace Roman space telescope highlights the urgency to upgrade our orbital infrastructure networks. The mission is expected to deliver 20,000 terabytes of data over its lifespan, as announced by NASA, which underscores the need for robust logistics networks and launch systems to manage this influx of information.

### **The EV Race**

A new 2025 electric vehicle (EV) landscape has emerged, where startup-focused brands like Tesla, Rivian, Lucid, and NIO abroad have prioritized "cleaner" EV-first platforms, forward-looking software, and charging ecosystems designed for seamless user experience. *What follows is purely a thought experiment.*

Imagine a future where NASA's Nancy Grace Roman space telescope, launched eight months ahead of schedule, has become the catalyst for a peculiar supply chain imbalance. As AI galaxy hunters pour over the 20,000 terabytes of data generated by the telescope, their computing demands have created an unprecedented surge in demand for high-performance GPUs. This unexpected twist has caused a ripple effect: Solid-State Battery manufacturers like QuantumScape and CATL, previously focused on electric vehicles, are now scrambling to pivot towards AI infrastructure, further exacerbating the global GPU crunch. Meanwhile, the UK's battery supply chain gap widens, as its EV-focused startups, such as Rivian, struggle to adapt to this new market reality, while Tesla, with its own AI-driven navigation systems, seizes the opportunity to corner the market on high-performance GPUs and AI-optimized batteries.
