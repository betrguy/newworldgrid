## Frontier Brief — Mar 24, 2026

**The High Ground**

SpaceX's successful landing of Starship HLS marks a significant step towards lunar human presence. This achievement reduces the complexity and cost associated with establishing a permanent lunar base, making it more feasible for commercial interests to invest in lunar infrastructure. With Starship HLS, SpaceX can now transfer humans to the Moon with greater efficiency, paving the way for the development of lunar resource utilization and space-based manufacturing.

**The Terrestrial Engine**

The recent tax on electric vehicles in China has sent shockwaves through the industry. BYD, a leading EV manufacturer, has issued a warning about the impact of this new tax, which could increase production costs by up to 20%. This move highlights the ongoing competition between Chinese and US-based EV manufacturers, with China's lead in battery technology and economies of scale under threat. The US, meanwhile, continues to invest heavily in EV research and development, with companies like Rivian and Lucid competing for market share.

**Frontier Speculation: The 0.1% Shift**

What if the recent advancements in sodium-ion batteries and perovskite solar technology were to converge into a single, high-efficiency energy storage system? This could enable the development of wirelessly charged spacecraft that can travel between Earth and lunar orbit without the need for traditional recharging infrastructure. Such a breakthrough would revolutionize the economics of space exploration and enable the creation of sustainable, off-world resource extraction industries.
