### **The High Ground**

Starlink’s missile tracker has reportedly been shut down, according to CBS News, as Russia launched new air strikes. This disruption could impact Ukraine’s ability to track incoming missiles, highlighting the critical role of space-based systems in modern warfare. Additionally, Musk stated that any limits on Ukraine's Starlink access are set by the U.S., not SpaceX itself. Ukraine reports that Starlink access has improved but is still facing speed limits, which are disrupting its battlefield operations.

### **The EV Race**

A new development in EV infrastructure has been reported. According to [ev market] news, the Arizona Department of Transportation (ADOT) has completed the installation of its first EV charging stations, but none are planned for Graham and Greenlee counties. This information comes from GilaValleyCentral.
