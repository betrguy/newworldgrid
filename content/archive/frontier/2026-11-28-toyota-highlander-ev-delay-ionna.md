### **The High Ground**

Rocket Lab’s latest Electron launch has propelled the company’s stock 5% higher. Meanwhile, SpaceX, which received backing from Cathie Wood, has risen 3%, indicating strong investor confidence. AST SpaceMobile has gained 2% despite a price target cut to $30. The electric propulsion satellite market is expected to reach US$ 148.78, reflecting growth in the sector.

### **The EV Race**

Tesla, Toyota, and General Motors are competing for a 53% share of the U.S. electric vehicle market in 2026. According to CarbonCredits.com, Tesla's sales fell by 47% in 2026. The Ford Bronco EV is priced at $32,320 in China but has no U.S. launch date, suggesting a focus on international markets. BYD, China’s leading EV manufacturer, recently launched a Rolls-Royce competitor in China, showcasing its ability to innovate and compete with luxury brands.
