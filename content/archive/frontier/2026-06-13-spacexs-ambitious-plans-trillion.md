### **The High Ground**

SpaceX's focus on building a $2 trillion company emphasizes the importance of orbital infrastructure in their "Orbital Economy" concept. According to SpaceX's plans, Launch Throughput And Orbital Infrastructure are crucial components. The company is also developing AI Data Centers and wireless connectivity as part of this vision.

### **The EV Race**

According to MotorWatt's recent data, BYD takes the top spot in search volume among top 5 EV brands with an index of 100. Rivian ranks second with an index of 62, followed by Hyundai and Kia. Tesla comes fourth with an index of 88.

Additionally, Nio has recently announced a 150kWh solid-state EV battery, expanding the options for buyers and manufacturers alike.

### **Frontier Speculation: The 0.1% Shift**

*What follows is purely a thought experiment.* Imagine a world 7 years from now where SpaceX's Starlink broadband constellation has created a satellite-based lithium-ion battery recycling ecosystem, leveraging its vast array of satellites to monitor and manage battery waste globally. CATL, in partnership with Hyundai, has set up a massive recycling facility in Indonesia, utilizing the data insights gathered by Starlink to optimize the recycling process. This synergy sparks a chain reaction: Tesla, sensing an opportunity, acquires a majority stake in Rivian's battery production lines, instantly becoming the largest electric vehicle manufacturer in the world. With its newfound scale, Tesla then uses its market dominance to pressure governments worldwide into adopting stricter regulations on EV battery recycling and responsible mining practices. This sudden shift in global policy forces Nio to abandon its solid-state battery ambitions and pivot towards a sustainable, recycled lithium-based approach, effectively doubling its production capacity and cementing its position as the top-tier premium EV brand.
