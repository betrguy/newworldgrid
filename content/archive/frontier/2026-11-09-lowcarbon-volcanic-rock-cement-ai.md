### **The High Ground**

China launched its 29th batch of internet satellites, continuing its expansion into the space-based internet market. This launch adds to the country's orbital infrastructure, potentially challenging existing satellite constellations.

### **The EV Race**

Ford’s new 30% more efficient EV platform, as reported by Notebookcheck on November 8, 2026, is shaking up the market. This advancement could shift buyer preferences towards Ford vehicles, offering better range and performance. As Barclays added Nio, XPeng, and Rivian in Q3, while cutting Tesla, Lucid, and GM shares, it suggests that investors are also showing interest in these companies, indicating a competitive landscape in the electric vehicle sector.

*What follows is purely a thought experiment.*

Imagine a scenario 7 years hence, where Nihility, a secretive artificial intelligence startup with ties to China, has developed an innovative satellite constellation. This constellation, named "ShadowNet," isn't merely a communication or navigation tool but a sophisticated surveillance network capable of high-resolution imaging and real-time data analysis. With the launch of its 29th batch of satellites, China has now established a near-perfect orbital surveillance coverage over key regions, including potential hotspots like the Taiwan Strait and the South China Sea.

This shift in capability is a game changer. Not only does it enhance China's strategic intelligence gathering, but it also poses a significant threat to global stability. Nations reliant on space-based assets, like the United States and India, must now contend with the possibility of their military and economic activities being monitored in unprecedented detail. The economic implications are vast: insurance premiums for satellites and space launches will skyrocket as risk assessments become more complex. Moreover, international relations could deteriorate as countries feel their sovereignty is being infringed upon, leading to increased tensions and potential conflicts over space law and regulation.
