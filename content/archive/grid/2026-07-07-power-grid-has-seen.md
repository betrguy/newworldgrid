<p class="meridian-lede">The New World Grid's State of the Grid report highlights a critical inflection point: the rapid expansion of battery storage and small modular reactors is redefining the grid's ability to integrate intermittent renewables and ensure reliable low-carbon power delivery. The US grid has added the equivalent of 20 nuclear reactors in battery storage capacity over the past four years, underscoring the need for enhanced resilience measures to mitigate vulnerabilities.</p>

The recent FERC reports emphasize the importance of addressing the pressing issue of grid security as the US grid continues to rely on outdated infrastructure.
