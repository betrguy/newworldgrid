<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">As the global energy landscape evolves, a critical shift is underway in the US grid infrastructure. The recent 2025 State of the Markets report by the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission highlights the need for enhanced grid resilience to mitigate potential cyber and physical shocks. This imperative is underscored by the ongoing buildout of small modular reactors (SMRs), which will provide reliable, low-carbon power.</p>

The integration of SMRs into the grid underscores the importance of robust infrastructure security measures to safeguard against transmission bottlenecks and reserve margin vulnerabilities.
