<p class="meridian-lede">The global energy landscape has undergone a significant transformation with the rapid growth of renewable energy adoption and corresponding battery storage capacity expansion. The stark contrast between these regional disparities underscores the need for policymakers to prioritize critical infrastructure protection and diversification.
