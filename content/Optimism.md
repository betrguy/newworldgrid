Today's progress reveals that biological systems fold proteins faster than previously thought, and that the relationship between sunspot cycle amplitudes and linear relations with white light facular areas is surprisingly consistent.

### Real-World Implications

- Researchers can now predict sunspot cycle amplitudes with accuracy within +/- 4% rms, given a stable source of continuum full disk photospheric images.
- The Baochip-1x SOC offers an objective, physically-based predictor of cycle amplitudes 3-4 years ahead of their maxima, for high-assurance applications.
- The nanosecond light-by-light switching in liquid crystal droplet could enable faster and more energy-efficient devices.
- The Kinetic Route to Helicity-Constrained Decay provides a source-compensated, history-dependent helicity density that satisfies an exact local balance identity by construction.
- The Layered Governance Architecture (LGA) for Autonomous Agent Systems proposes a four-layer framework for execution sandboxing, intent verification, zero-trust inter-agent authorization, and immutable audit logging.
- The recent advancements in protein folding and women's health may lead to better diagnostics and treatments for women, by disaggregating data by sex.
- The breakthroughs in materials science could lead to new technologies for computing and communication.
