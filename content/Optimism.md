<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">Researchers at the U.S. Department of Energy's Ames National Laboratory have discovered a method to manipulate magnons, the tiny magnetic waves that can transmit information. This breakthrough, with its potential to enhance quantum computing, aligns with a new perovskite system that extends spin polarization at room temperature, opening doors for future spin-based applications.</p>

This dual advancement in controlling magnetic behavior and preserving spin information significantly advances our ability to develop more efficient and robust quantum technologies. For the first time, scientists can precisely direct these magnetic waves, a capability that was once out of reach. This means that in the near future, devices leveraging quantum properties could become more practical and reliable, bringing us closer to a new era of computing power.
</div>
