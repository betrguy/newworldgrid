<div class="energy-pulse">
<p class="meridian-lede">With the development of quantum chips that can hold multiple photons at once, storing quantum information is no longer a bottleneck. This advancement, combined with the ability to link smaller photon groups into larger entangled states, paves the way for more powerful and scalable quantum computers.</p>

These developments mean that the constraints of fragile quantum information systems are significantly reduced. Now, quantum computers can process information more robustly, leading to breakthroughs in fields like cryptography, drug discovery, and complex system simulation. The specific barrier of photon storage, which has been a limiting factor, is no longer an impediment, opening a path to more reliable and efficient quantum technologies.
